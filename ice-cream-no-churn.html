No churn ice cream

Fruit

  1. Blitz
    • 200g mango
    • 198g sweetened condensed milk
    • zest & juice of 1 lime

Ice cream

  1. Whisk to soft peaks
    • 300ml whipping cream
  2. Mix carefully with big spoon till blended in
    • fruit purée
  3. Spoon into tub
  4. Freeze overnight
