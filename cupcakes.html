Cupcakes

Notes

  • Preheat oven to 170°C
  • Use butter, eggs and milk at room temperature

Cupcakes

  1. Cream in bowl then whisk for 3-5 mins
    • 110g butter
    • 110g caster sugar
  2. Whisk in
    • 2 large eggs beaten
    • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  3. Fold in
    • 110g self-raising flour sifted
  4. Mix in gently
    • 1-2 tbsp (15-30g) milk
  5. Half fill 9 cupcake cases
  6. Bake at 170°C for 12-15 mins
