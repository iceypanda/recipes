Sun-dried tomatoes

  2. Blitz

Paella

  1. Heat for 10 mins
    • olive oil
    • 1 onion chopped
    • 1 red pepper sliced long
    • 1 yellow pepper sliced long
    • 1 carrot diced (optional)
  2. Add and cook for 2 mins
    • 3 cloves garlic grated
  3. Add and mix well
    • 250g paella rice
    • large pinch saffron
    • 1½ tsp sweet smoked paprika
    • 1½ tsp paprika
    • ¼ tsp mild chilli powder
  4. Add and simmer for 25 mins till rice is cooked
    • 750ml stock (1 salted & 1 unsalted cube)
    • sun-dried tomatoes
  5. Mix in and cook for 5 mins
    • 150g peas
    • 250g uncooked prawns
  6. Add
    • 2 tsp lemon juice
    • parsely chopped
    • salt to taste

Serving
