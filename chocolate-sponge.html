Chocolate orange sponge

Bowl

  1. Grease 1100ml (medium) Pyrex bowl with 10g butter
  2. Place small circle of baking paper at bottom of bowl to make it easier to remove the sponge

Sponge

  1. Whisk till well combined
    • 100g unsalted butter cubed & softened
    • 100g self-raising floor
    • 100g caster sugar
    • 2 medium eggs
    • 40g cocoa powder
    • 4 tbsp water cold
    • ½ tsp baking powder
  2. Mix in
    • zest of ½ orange
  3. Cook in microwave at 1000W for 3½ mins
  4. Leave to cool
  5. Turn the pudding onto a plate

Serving

  • Chocolate sauce
  • 4 portions
