Mushroom risotto

Mushroom stock

  1. Soak for 20 mins
    • 1L water boiled
    • 30g porcini mushrooms
    • 1 stock cube
  2. Remove porcini

Risotto

  1. Fry for 8 mins
    • 2 small (180g) onions chopped
    • olive oil
  2. Add and fry for 1 min
    • 2 cloves garlic
  3. Add and cook for 8 mins
    • 300g chestnut mushrooms chopped
    • 100g shitaki mushrooms chopped (optional)
    • porcini chopped
  4. Add and cook for 1 min
    • 250g arborio rice
  5. Add and evapourate the alcohol
    • 90g white wine
  6. Pour stock in batches till gone, stirring regularly
  7. Add water till rice is cooked
  8. Add
    • 40g parmesan grated
    • 25g butter
    • spinach stalks removed
    • parsely
    • juice ½ lemon
    • salt (if unsalted stock)

Serving

  • 4 portions
  • Salmon
