Chocolate hazelnut oaty bites

  • Preheat oven to 180°C
  • Use large saucepan so do not need extra mixing bowl
  1. Heat gently to simmer
    • 125ml (115g) olive oil
    • 125g coconut sugar
    • 60g water
    • 3 tbsp (60g) maple syrup
    • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  2. Remove heat
  3. Stir in and mix well
    • 350g oats
    • 75g dark chocolate chopped fine
    • 25g hazelnuts roasted & chopped fine
  4. Line tin with butter and baking paper
  5. Spoon mixture into 20x20cm baking tin and smooth the surface with wooden spoon at flat angle
  6. Sprinkle with
    • 25g dark chocolate chopped
    • 25g hazelnuts unroasted & roughly chopped
  7. Bake at 180°C for 25 mins
  8. Leave to cool for 20 mins
  9. Cut into segments
  10. Leave to cool completely

Serving

  • 16 portions
