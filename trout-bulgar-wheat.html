Trout & bulgar wheat

Veg

  1. Cook till soft
    • 200g mushrooms chopped
    • 1 yellow/red pepper chopped
    • 2 carrots thin half moons
    • 1 clove garlic minced
    • olive oil
  2. Add
    • ½ tsp soy sauce
    • ½ tsp marmite (optional)

Trout & bulgar wheat

  1. Mix in bowl
    • 140g bulgar wheat cooked
    • 1 avocado sliced
    • veg
    • lemon juice
  2. Serve with
    • hot-smoked trout

Serving

  • 3 portions
