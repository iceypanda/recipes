Kalamata olive, tuna, & feta pasta

Prep

  • Rinse and add back to jar
    • 1 jar kalamata olives destoned & halved

On hob

  1. Fry till soft
    • olive oil
    • 2 medium onions
    • 3 cloves garlic
  2. Add and simmer for 10 mins
    • 2 tins of tomatoes
    • 8 (200g) mushrooms chopped
    • 1 tbsp tomato puree
    • 1 tbsp basil
    • 1 tbsp oregano
    • 6 bay leaves
  3. Add and simmer for 10 mins
    • 2 courgettes chopped into semi-circles
  4. Add and simmer for 10 mins
    • kalamata olives
  5. Add and simmer for 10 mins
    • 1 large tin tuna
  6. Add
    • 1 pack feta cubed
