Winter tomato soup

Dried tomatoes

  1. Leave to soaked in sauce pan
    • 80g sun-dried tomatoes (not oil)
    • 500ml water boiling
    • 1 tsp sugar
  2. Remove soaked tomatoes and blitz

Tinned tomatoes

  1. Drain liquid into sun-dried tomato pan
    • 2 tins whole peeled tomatoes halved
  2. Mix tomatoes with
    • 2 tbsp olive oil
    • 1 tsp thyme
  3. Roast at 190°C for 30 mins
  4. Roughly chop

Soup

  1. Cook for 5 mins
    • 2 tbsp olive oil
    • 1 red onion thinly sliced
    • 1 carrot diced
  2. Add and cook for 2 mins
    • 1 tbsp garlic chopped
  3. Add and bring to boil then simmer for 35 mins
    • 800ml stock (1 no salt cube + ½ stock pot)
    • liquid from sun-dried tomatoes
    • soaked tomatoes
    • roasted tomatoes & juices
  4. Season to taste with salt and pepper
  5. Blitz till smooth

Serving

  • Stilton & fig wholemeal scones
  • Bacon & parmesan biscuits

Notes

  • Made: 2 Jan 2023, too salty with one salt stock pot
