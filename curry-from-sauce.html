Curry from sauce

Spices

  • Cardamom pods: replace with scant ¼ tsp
  • Cloves: replace with 1 ground clove
  • Peppercorns: grind

Veg

  1. Roast at 200°C
    • squash 40 mins
    • aubergine 30 mins, stripped
    • sweet potato 30 mins
    • pepper 30 mins
    • mushroom 15 mins

Curry

  1. Fry for 5 mins
    • 2 medium onions
  2. Add and cook for 5 mins
    • 400-500g chicken
  3. Add and cook for 3 mins
    • sauce 1
  4. Add and cook for 10 mins
    • sauce 2
    • 200-250g coconut milk / passata
    • 1 tsp cornflour in water
    • roasted veg

Serving

  • 4-5 portions
