Chicken fajitas

Spices

  • Mix
    • 1½ tbsp smoked paprika
    • 1 tbsp ground coriander
    • ½ tsp mild chilli powder
    • ½ tsp cumin
    • ½ tsp salt

Garlic

  • Grate
    • 4 cloves garlic

Peppers

  1. Mix in large bowl
    • 1 red onion thinly sliced
    • 1 yellow pepper thinly sliced
    • 2 sweet red peppers thinly sliced
  2. Mix in
    • ½ garlic
    • ½ spices
  3. Mix in
    • 1 tbsp olive oil
  4. Roast in air fryer at 180°C for 13 mins

Fajitas

  1. Mix in bowl used for the peppers
    • 400-500g chicken breast sliced
    • ½ garlic
    • ½ spices
  2. Mix in
    • 1 tbsp olive oil
  3. Place on top of peppers
  4. Roast at 180°C for 13 mins
  5. Leave to rest for 5 mins
  6. Mix in
    • juice and zest 1 lime
  7. Cut chicken into thin slices

Serving

  • 3-4 portions
  • tortillas warmed
  • sour cream / natural yogurt
  • cheddar cheese grated
  • Pico de gallo
  • lettuce
