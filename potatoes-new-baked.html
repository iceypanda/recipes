Baked new potatoes

Notes

  • Do not need to parboil before baking
  • Potatoes will go slimy inside if overcooked

Prep

  1. Coat with any of
    • jerk seasoning
    • garlic salt
    • paprika
  2. Mix with
    • olive oil

Baking

Air fryer

  1. Roast at 190°C for 22 mins

Oven

  1. Roast in oven at 200°C for 25-30 mins till golden

Varieties

  • Celandine - not good for baking, sweet and slimy when cooked (but were parboiled first so maybe too much)
